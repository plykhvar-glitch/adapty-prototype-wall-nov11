# Adapty Prototype Wall

**Live:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/adapty-prototype-wall-nov11/

An event page to show prospects their own prototype. Tap a company and its live onboarding and paywall prototype becomes a full-screen QR code; the lead scans it and opens the prototype on their phone. Swipe for the next company. The chips at the top filter the cards by owner.

Open one company straight away by adding its key to the link, e.g. `#babbel`, `#norton`, `#structured`. On an iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen opens it full-screen like an app.

Rebuild after adding a company: edit `APPS` in `build.py`, then run it with the London wall's `index.html` as the argument.
